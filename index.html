10-hour plan at a glance

Efficiency is the share of paid hours that land on a covered hour of the clock. With two 10-hour shifts back to back there is no turnover overlap; the rest of the paid hours go to relief days, which cover vacation and sick leave. Days worked and days off are per person over a 52-week year (364 days); the year view shows the exact count for your 365 days.

Relief days (outlined) are spare capacity: the relief person covers whoever is on leave, and works as an extra if no one is out. Weekday labels and weekend shading follow the real calendar for the start date you pick. The start date defaults to January 1 of next year. Each line is anchored to the weekday, so weekends stay balanced whatever date you pick. Pay periods start on the cycle start date.
